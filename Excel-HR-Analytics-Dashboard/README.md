# HR Analytics Dashboard in Excel

A simple, interactive dashboard that shows **who works at a company, who leaves, and how much they are paid**. Built fully in Microsoft Excel.

## What is this project?

A company wants to know: *How many employees are leaving? Are they happy? Is pay fair?*
This dashboard answers those questions in **one screen**. We can click the filters (slicers) and every chart updates instantly.

## The main numbers

|||
|-|-|
|**Total employees**|1,470|
|**Current employees**|1,233|
|**Employees who left**|237|
|**Attrition rate**|19.22% *(left ÷ current employees, as shown on the dashboard)*|
|**Gender (current staff)**|59% male, 41% female|

## What We can see on the dashboard

* **Gender:** how many men and women work here
* **Education level:** from "Below College" up to "Doctor"
* **Employee satisfaction:** how happy people are with their jobs
* **Work-life balance:** Bad, Good, Better or Best
* **Salary by tenure:** how pay grows for Contractors and Full-time staff the longer they stay
* **Male vs. female salary by experience:** how pay compares across years of experience

## Filters We can click

Gender · Employment Type · Department · Education · Job Role

## Interesting things I found in the data

* People who work **overtime** leave **3 times more** (30.5% vs. 10.4%).
* People who **left** earned less on average (about 4,787 vs. 6,833).
* **Contractors** leave more often than full-time staff (22% vs. 15%).
* People with a **bad work-life balance** leave the most (31%).

*(These come from the dataset itself, not from the dashboard charts.)*

## How to open it

1. Download this repository (green **Code** button → **Download ZIP**).
2. Open `dashboard/HR\_Analytics\_Dashboard.xlsx` in **Microsoft Excel** (2016 or newer).
3. Go to the **Dashbord** sheet and click the filters.
